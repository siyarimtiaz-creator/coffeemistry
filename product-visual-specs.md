# Coffeemistry Temporary Product Visual Specification

## Shared art direction

Each temporary visual is an internal, **photorealistic placeholder visual**, not a photograph of the physical Coffeemistry product. Produce a 4:5 portrait commercial food photograph with a single centered product, warm cinematic specialty-cafe lighting, espresso-brown atmosphere, cream highlights, shallow depth of field, refined natural shadows, and generous negative space. Use no people, text, labels, logos, watermarks, packaging brands, clipart, illustration, or exaggerated AI styling. Preserve natural proportions and appetizing textures.
